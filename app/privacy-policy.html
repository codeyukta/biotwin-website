Bio Twin Health

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Bio Twin Health ("Bio Twin," "we," "us," or "our") is a personal health-tracking app that helps you log meals, medicines, activity, and sleep, and turns that information — together with data you connect from Apple Health / Health Connect — into personalized insights. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how you can control or delete it.

Not a medical device or medical advice. Bio Twin Health is an informational wellness tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and its AI-generated insights are not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider about your health, medications, or any symptoms — including anything Bio Twin Health flags for you.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, we receive your name, email address, and a unique account identifier. We use this to create and secure your account. We never receive or store your Google/Apple password.

Health and fitness data you connect

With your permission, we read the following categories from Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Health Connect (Android):

  • Steps, active and total calories burned, activity intensity
  • Heart rate and resting heart rate
  • Sleep sessions
  • Height, weight, hydration
  • Blood glucose and blood pressure (where your device or a connected source provides them)

On iOS, we may also write workout data back to HealthKit when you log an exercise in the app, so it stays in sync with the rest of your Health data.

Information you enter yourself

  • Logged activities — meals, medicines and dosages, exercise, sleep, and notes you record through the chat-based logging assistant.
  • Medical documents — lab reports or diagnostic documents you photograph or upload. We use on-device text recognition to extract values such as glucose, HbA1c, cholesterol, or blood pressure from these documents.
  • Chat messages — your conversation with the in-app health assistant, including any voice input you convert to text.
  • Profile details — weight and other configuration values you provide for personalization.

2. How we use your information

  • To build your personal health baseline ("BioPrint") and show trends in your dashboard.
  • To generate personalized, AI-assisted insights and replies in the chat assistant.
  • To keep your data in sync across your devices via your account.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the app, and to fix bugs.

We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Health data specifically (HealthKit & Health Connect)

In accordance with Apple's and Google's platform requirements:

  • Health data obtained through HealthKit or Health Connect is used only to provide health, fitness, or wellness features within the app — never for advertising or other data-mining purposes unrelated to your health.
  • We do not share HealthKit or Health Connect data with third parties such as advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • Where a summarized version of this data is sent to an AI provider (Groq/OpenRouter) to generate an insight or reply, that use is limited to producing the response you asked for, as described in Section 3.

5. How long we keep your data

We retain your account and logged data for as long as your account is active, so your history and trends remain available to you. If you stop using the app without deleting your account, your data remains stored under your account until you delete it or request deletion.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app: Profile → My Account → Delete Account. This will:

  • Permanently delete your logged meals, medicines, activities, sleep entries, chat history, uploaded document data, and health metrics from our servers.
  • Delete your account record and sign you out.
  • Clear the data cached locally on your device.

This action cannot be undone. If you're unable to use the in-app option, you can request deletion by emailing us at the address in Section 12 — we will act on it within 30 days.

7. Security

Data in transit between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Your logged data is stored under access rules that restrict it to your own account. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw previously given consent (for example, by disconnecting Health/Health Connect access in your device settings). You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app, or by contacting us using the details in Section 12.

9. Children's privacy

Bio Twin Health is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. International data transfers

Our service providers may process and store data on servers located outside your country of residence. Where this occurs, we rely on our providers' own safeguards for cross-border data transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
